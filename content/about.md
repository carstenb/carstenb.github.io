Carsten Bokemeyer is an experienced Product Guy and Agile Enthusiast working in Bonn.

He commenced his professional career at [SCHWIND. Werbeagentur](https://www.schwind.de) a Marketing Agency in Bonn, where he was working as a Consultant in the New Media department. At the same time, he successfully attended an evening course in marketing communication at the Westdeutsche Akademie für Kommunikation in Cologne.

In 2009, Carsten Bokemeyer switched into a corporate environment in order to further develop his skills in the field of online marketing. He started as an Online Marketing Manager at Studitemps GmbH and attended a second evening course at the Westdeutsche Akademie für Kommunikation in Cologne, this time with focus on online marketing.

Within [Studitemps GmbH](https://www.studitemps.de), Carsten Bokemeyer had the chance to change into the role as Product Manager for the student portal [jobmensa.de](https://www.jobmensa.de), due to his previously gained theoretical and practical experiences. While working as a Product Manager, he discovered his passion for agile product development.

In order to broaden his knowledge in the fields of Scrum, Product Ownership, Agile Practices as well as Leadership, he worked in a variety of roles including among others Product Manager, Product Owner, Scrum Master as well as Team Lead. In 2011, Carsten was assigned as Head of Product. His initial goal was to establish a diversified product development team by setting up and supervising four cross-functional Scrum teams.

In 2018 he took on a new challenge as Product Owner at Chefkoch GmbH, the company behind the biggest recipe plattform in the german-speaking area - [chefkoch.de](https://www.chefkoch.de). There he is responsible for the complete mobile experience on the plattforms iOS and Android.
